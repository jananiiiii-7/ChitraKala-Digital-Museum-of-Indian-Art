# KALĀKRITI | Digital Museum of Indian Art History

**Welcome to KALĀKRITI**, an immersive virtual museum celebrating over two millennia of Indian artistic heritage, master craftsmen, sacred tribal traditions, and iconic movements.

---

## 🏛️ Digital Museum Overview

`KALĀKRITI` provides a digital museum experience powered by real geographic map technology (Leaflet.js) and curated museum exhibition halls. Visitors can explore 15 master art hubs across India, inspect high-resolution artwork visuals, listen to museum audio guides, explore step-by-step master crafting techniques, and uncover fascinating fun facts and historical trivia.

## 🚀 How to Launch the Digital Museum

1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any web browser.
2. **Interactive Museum Features**:
   - **Real Interactive Map**: Powered by Leaflet.js dark map tiles; click any museum pin to fly directly to the location!
   - **Curator Notes & Fun Facts**: Inspect intriguing trivia bullet points and historical context for every masterpiece.
   - **Audio Guide**: Click "Audio Guide" in the top bar to listen to the museum curator speak notes out loud using Web Speech synthesis!
   - **Time Travel Timeline Slider**: Filter master exhibits chronologically from 2nd Century BCE to the present day.
